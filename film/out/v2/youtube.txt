Title: HandoffProof — Rehearse the day your developer leaves (Meta AI Hackathon Demo)

Description:
Your only developer is leaving Friday. What breaks on Monday?

HandoffProof rehearses the exit before it happens: it runs your real workflows in a Docker sandbox, withdraws the developer's credentials, removes configuration, tests the backup — and records exactly what broke. A deterministic Go controller decides pass/fail; the Meta model (muse-spark-1.3, Responses API) only proposes a map, a diagnosis with verified file:line citations, and a repair that is believed only after a green rerun.

This is an animated walkthrough with recreated application screens, based on recorded demo artifacts. It illustrates the withdrawn-token scenario, evidence and diagnosis views, repair/rerun workflow, application onboarding, and Slack/GitHub interfaces. The benchmark figures refer to the recorded demo evaluation: static 3/5, drills 4/5, full HandoffProof 5/5, second fixture 3/3. It is not a recording of a live drill or a live integration connection.
